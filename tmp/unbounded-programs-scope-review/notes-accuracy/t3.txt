T3-tool-hard-limits T3 — Hard (non-resource) limits in the pinned toolchain (GNAT 16.1 frontend/backend, GNATprove 16.1/Why3/CVC5/Z3 4.15.4/Alt-Ergo 2.6.1) and Linux OS
I probed with gcc -gnatc and full -O0 compiles, gnatprove in check and flow mode, four tiny --mode=all runs to read the effective prover limits, kernel exec probes, and source reading of GCC 16.1 (gcc-mirror 6afcc4f6) and the spark2014/why3 producer (42554fc2). I found four limits that make an admitted program fail other than by resource exhaustion:
(1) GNAT physical line width. After expanding each tab to the next multiple of 8 columns, a line can be at most 32,765 columns. 32,765 bytes passes and 32,766 fails, with or without a terminator. With tabs, a 4,131-byte line already fails.
(2) Identifier length is at most 4,096 characters. 4,097 gives "GNAT BUG DETECTED Program_Error namet.adb:141 / Name buffer overflow; Max_Length = 4096" in every tested role and mode. This comes from namet.ads:144, where Bounded_String defaults to 2**12.
(3) Total source size near 2^31 bytes. 2^31-2^24 passes (10.5 s, 2.16 GB). 2^31-1 crashes with SIGSEGV and heap corruption. 2^31 makes gnat1 loop forever inside errout.
(4) In the proof configuration, --level=2 makes --memlimit=0 impossible: every prover process keeps a 1,000 MiB RLIMIT_AS (observed in why3session.xml). Dropping --level gives memlimit 0.
Also: Z3's rlimit wraps modulo 2^32, so any finite --steps above 5,368,146 silently gives Z3 a tiny limit. Prover crashes ('High failure'), including resource kills, are reported as 'gave_up', the same as a genuine 'unknown'.
Everything else I measured is resource-only: nesting depth, declaration and statement counts, total size up to 2 GB, backend size, and debug lines past 1.25M. The costs are stack, memory and time, mostly superlinear: gnatc time is quadratic in sequence length and nesting, and flow memory reaches 4.4 GB at 1 MB and 6.5 GB at 8,000 nested loops. Stack overflow appears as a GNAT bug box. gcc raises its own stack limit; gnat2why inherits 8 MiB and fails in flow mode between 1,000 and 2,000 nested loops (check mode between 2,000 and 8,000). All of these pass with an unlimited stack. OS limits are environmental: a single argument can be at most 131,071 bytes (one byte more gives E2BIG), and ARG_MAX is 2 MiB, set as stack/4.
