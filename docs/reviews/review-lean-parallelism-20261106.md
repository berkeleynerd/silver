## Review finding and disposition

The first predicate split placed many formerly separate theorem proofs inside
four `checked` commands, including 108 fields in the body leaf. This needlessly
changed declaration/resource boundaries during a scheduling optimization.
Pinned Lean's `CoreM.lean` describes `maxHeartbeats` as a per-command limit;
`Command.runCore` initializes that context. No timeout was observed or predicted
as certain. In particular, `decide +kernel` still creates individually checked
auxiliary lemmas, so this was not a loss of kernel checking or one monolithic
reduction.

The lead and reviewer agreed to preserve the original private declarations and
use reference-only bundles. Commit `9100bb2fca` implements that repair; the
incremental review confirmed unchanged bundle propositions, assembler, decision
inventory and original declaration bytes. The prior `import all Init.Data.Repr`
was relocated from the original candidate into the decision-bearing leaves;
it does not introduce a new trusted assumption.
