## Checked argument

**Wrapper counts apply to one escaping value, not all sequential work.** The
fixed selected root/registry relation is essential. The twenty transformations,
fixed flag callbacks and four later libraries do not enable arbitrary plugin,
strategy, argument-transform or sexp conversion callbacks. Their accepted
inventories were reused rather than recreated. New roots would invalidate this
closure until separately checked.

`Trans.named` supplies the ordinary outer layer. The explicit/default selectors
in encoding and discrimination supply at most two more. The discrimination
selectors and driver transformation lists are sequential; an escaping error
does not pass through subsequent sibling selectors. The actual flag callbacks
do not recursively invoke a new selector registry. This supports three layers,
including failures during setup that occur before an outer named wrapper exists.

Loc's ordinary helpers coalesce an outer Located. The two clone-statement
handlers do not coalesce, but find_module executes before their protected
clone block. Nested library-read failure therefore crosses coalescing helpers,
not an unbounded series of these clone handlers. The protected clone copies
units/maps; its Uclone arm does not parse another clone statement. Two location
layers are conservative for this selected creation route.

The single BadI_rs_type construction protects `e_exec(c_app ...)`. The result
is Capp, so it does not enter Cfun's expression-body branch; its contract/type
operations have no parser/clone callback that could construct another nested
BadI_rs_type. The source exception identity is Theory.BadInstance even though
the creating site is in Pmodule and the recursive printer is in Pretty.

Fun.protect wraps a failing cleanup, not the original work exception. Successful
cleanup reraises work unchanged; another cleanup failure replaces it. The
selected timing and file cleanups do not invoke protect recursively. The
pending signal save goes directly to session save and has no extra timing
protect around it. This addresses nested signals as well as ordinary cleanup;
it does not assume cleanup or save succeeds. The sum 3+2+1+1=7 is conservative,
without claiming simultaneous attainability of all maxima.

**The recurrence charges fallback after a partially printed prefix.** Why's
registry advances after failure with the original exception. Its Printexc
adapter performs one registered pass and does not call the anomaly handler.
Printexc also keeps its original exception while advancing callbacks. A single
recursive constructor has one matching branch per Why pass. Thus a package has
at most two strict-inner packages, with Finally's one call below that allowance.
`N(d)=1+2N(d-1)`, N(0)=1, gives N(7)=255. Two Why passes per package give
510 passes and 28,050 candidates; five Printexc callbacks give 1,275 attempts.
The particular terminal matching branch has <=256 attempts, while Pretty's
rejecting and wrapper-level attempts require the larger 510 bound. The exact
accepted outer counts 392/326/200/26/14 multiply these interfaces without a new
signal budget. Eight simultaneous value packages are not a native-stack bound.
