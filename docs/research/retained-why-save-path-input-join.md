## What closes the former path-input gap

The [initial-state argument](retained-why-path-initial-state.md) excludes a
resumable pre-cut Theory mutation at each actual cut. The accepted current-array
census consequently covers all initially selectable values for the serialized
tags: nonempty paths have at most six bytes and one component. Unrelated current
payloads need not be decoded because their immutable cached hashes cannot turn
into a matching hash merely through a later resize. Previously abandoned arrays
cannot resume as a table continuation.

The [later-writer argument](retained-why-path-library-origins.md) closes named
library selection and covers the selected preparation, printer, result, error
and pending-client routes. It allows tag collisions and foreign expression-body
cache hits: the bounded invariant concerns declaration heads, type-definition
graphs and namespace units. All later installed paths are empty, a bounded tuple
path, or a path from the four-file parser/clone family. Their bound remains
`3*38,678+128 = 116,162` under normal numerical/allocation services. Counterexample
model-parser reads are excluded by the explicit immutable-meta argument.

Taking the maximum of the initial and later bounds gives R=116,162. Replacement,
reparsing, loss to fallback, collection and later partial resize do not require
the original binding to remain selected: every eligible installed payload is
bounded. The prior initial suspended-operation exclusion covers old pre-cut
roots; among payloads carried by later resumable mutations, those eligible for
the captured serialized tags are bounded initial/new values. Unrelated initial
cells remain ineligible because their cached hashes are immutable. The selected
finite immutable list/value relation is essential. This argument
does not derive runtime memory integrity from the numerical bound.

For N serialized identifier occurrences, C other raw string bytes and maximum
immutable fallback length F, the previously reviewed transfer gives

```text
U = C + N*max(F,R)
d = 20
B = 1024*Q + 6*U + 32*Q*d
E = 128*Q + U
```

N, C, F and Q are the retained process-specific occurrence/continuation bounds;
they are not extrapolated from another process. The accepted global save budget
is at most three over the entire selected post-cut interval, without restarting
at ordinary-save entry. The small initial identifier sums remain initial facts,
not whole-interval U values. Earlier packets' null actual inputs are preserved
as historical records.

## Evidence and limits

The [Round 32 packet](../evidence/retained-why-save-round32-20261020/README.md)
reconciles exact author inputs and applies the existing arithmetic interfaces.
Its `compose.py` preserves null usable inputs when any external premise is
absent, and keeps the complete-capacity flags false even when all premises are
supplied. The flags encode the reviewed source arguments and their conditions;
they do not machine-prove the semantic writer closure. The default does not
silently assume service success.

The scope is the five named cuts and their selected continuations, not every
past path in those processes or arbitrary Why3 invocations. Complete restore-table
lookup capacity, CamlinternalFormat accumulator construction, aggregate
allocation/native layout, stack/GC, numerical failure, channel/file offset,

and callback behavior relation remains explicit. The later driver/solver/server/
report transfers, full fixed-library and current-grammar coverage, decidable
whole-rule composition/correspondence, useful-domain comparison and final review
remain on #119.
