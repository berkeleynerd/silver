## Correction: mark phase and the contiguous arena

The old R52 timing note's arena paragraph calls captured phase0 idle and
infers no prior sweep from that label. **That reasoning is superseded here.**
The retained runtime defines mark=0 and idle=3. Frozen historical files are
preserved; use this corrected source argument for their arena premise.
There is no assertion that mark allocation behaves like idle allocation.

1. Runtime initialization creates a single free arena block: 2^24 words is
   below the runtime maximum block size. Its free-set representation may
   depend on allocation policy, but it starts with one contiguous block.
2. As R55 establishes, the added argv words cannot cause a collection before
   the same explicit `Re.Dense_map.make` startup collection: the maximum
   pre-collection minor total including the addition is 3,312, below 2^28.
   The quiet/base and driver edits are read later. That same explicit minor
   collection promotes the rooted argv addition, with at most four extra
   words, and enters `major_collection_slice` from idle with an empty minor
   arena. `major_gc.c:1084–1093` starts a mark cycle, sets p=0 and exits
   without `mark_slice`. Root metadata can differ; equality is unnecessary.
3. Consider the prefix up to a hypothetical first later collection/request,
   heap expansion or other free-set-changing service. Under the inherited
   source/service relation there is no explicit collection, compaction,
   policy switch or external free-set mutation. Starting the cycle does not
   sweep or reclaim blocks. Without a later slice the runtime cannot reach
   its clean/sweep transitions. Normal allocation consumes the one free
   remnant. In all three allocator policies it splits that remnant from one
   end; the remaining >8 million words exclude small-fragment cases.
   `memory.c:471–498` chooses the allocation color **after** selecting the
   free block. Mark makes new major blocks black; it does not create holes.
   `Obj.truncate` leaves a black Abstract-tagged remainder inside an already
   allocated major block, without returning it to the free set.
4. Induct on allocation requests including headers, charging retained
   startup history and all new credits. The total is at most 8,526,217,
   leaving at least **8,250,999 contiguous words** in the initial arena.
   Thus every charged request fits its remnant and the first expansion
   cannot occur. This uses the actual allocation history, not free-space
   totals in an arbitrary fragmented heap. The source exclusions and strict
   minor/major/table/resource margins exclude the other first-event routes.

Consequently the whole successful controlled prefix has no additional
collection, sweep, weak clearing or arena expansion. The ordinary constructor
and cache relation follows jointly with the first-event contradiction.
Write-barrier color changes need not be identical. The new FileTags tables
are young; their candidate buckets stay young without a collection, and
their major-array stores do not remove old scannable major contents. R55
separately handles the logger's overwritten stdout custom block. No new
mark-stack growth is inferred from those additions.
