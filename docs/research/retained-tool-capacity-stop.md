# Retained-tool capacity: whole-#119 research stop

2026-10-20, Linux x86_64. Parent checkpoint: `d19f73cb3f` (Round 46).

**The research has not established a complete capacity rule for the proposed
Silver grammar/domain. Its deliverable is the precise research-stop alternative
explicitly allowed by #119.** The accepted selected native rule and the
conditional producer/Why3 results remain reusable. This report identifies what
is missing, why the newest solver result does not close it, and the alternatives
available without pretending that an unproved bound is an impossibility result.

Acceptance of this stop requires the [independent review](../reviews/review-cvc5-output-closure-20261020.md)
of its exact frozen input, followed by integration and publication. That review
records its disposition separately; this document alone does not assert that
those steps have happened. A completed research-stop deliverable is not a
delivered capacity rule, implementation readiness, or permission to expand the
language. The Oracle, installed tools/pins and current admission stay unchanged.

## Contract and scope

The [#119 contract](https://github.com/berkeleynerd/silver/issues/119) permits
either a concrete computable rule with complete phase/construct coverage,
assumptions, soundness and comparison, independently reviewed, **or a precise
reviewed stop identifying unresolved obligations and alternatives**. The live
harness objective has the same alternative. Selecting it requires no new Oracle
amendment or implementation authorization. It does not assert that the owner
selected a different toolchain or abandoned the unchanged-toolchain direction.

The requested domain is the proposed relaxation of the currently supported
Silver constructs and their permitted compositions, with fixed dependencies
and assurance-essential production paths. It is not the full SPARK language.
The [inputs and required conclusion](retained-tool-capacity.md#inputs-and-required-conclusion)
require decisions from authoritative source/configuration/dependency bytes
before compiling or proving the application. Execution success, a captured
state, a table inventory, and a component bound are insufficient individually.

Representation capacity is distinct from available RAM/disk/time. Ordinary
allocator/runtime/OS correctness for valid requests remains trusted. That trust
does not establish Silver's caller arithmetic, lifetime, stack, output-payload
or capacity preconditions. Existing semantic safety, complete obligation
generation and automatic discharge requirements remain in force.

## Concrete solver-output gap

For the retained candidate CVC5 source and selected command family, one
`check-sat` can execute repeated full-effort arithmetic checks. Each rebuilds
the integer-model cache. A nonintegral model value can trigger a warning and a
new branch-and-bound lemma, after which checking returns normally to search.
The [diagnostic packet](retained-cvc5-diagnostic-output.md#scope-and-why-command-counts-do-not-close-this-family)
pins the complete control chain and the returning branch. Neither one query,
the input token count, nor the distinct input-variable count bounds cumulative
visits to this warning.

The warning has 72 fixed bytes plus a printed term and rational. Under the
specified SMT2 formatting contract, a rational needs at most its numerator and
denominator decimal digits plus nine bytes. Its GMP representation is not an
int64 value. For submitted task size N the required prospective functions are:

- `K_ar(N)`: cumulative warning-entry visits over every charged execution prefix;
- `L_ar(N)`: cumulative rendered term bytes, including repeated occurrences;
- `D_ar(N)`: cumulative numerator and denominator decimal digits.

They would give `W_ar <= 81*K_ar(N) + L_ar(N) + D_ar(N)`. All three bounds, or
an alternative source-corresponding invariant that the warning is unreachable
for the entire selected task family, remain unproved. There is no demonstrated
selected-input occurrence, divergence, representation overflow or impossibility
of a finite bound. A lexical warning census or a larger sample run cannot
replace the missing quantified argument.

The other Round 47 results narrow but do not remove the gap:

- The parser does not recover/rescan after an escaping parser exception in the
  selected noninteractive route. Nested terms cannot dispatch new top-level
  commands. Selected automatic option writers preserve the relevant defaults;
  malformed Float80 templates may still fail parsing. Parser-warning bytes have
  a task/filename interface, but rendered semantic operands `H` and caught
  exception payloads `E_caught` remain to be bounded or excluded.
- At most six terminal activations follow under the terminal packet's explicit
  single-thread, successful POSIX disposition/raise, nonlocal-recovery and saved
  nonreturning-handler premises. The direct-text term `8688+24P0` excludes safe
  statistics, debug exception buffers, preceding assertions and saved runtime
  handler text. Signal-point histogram/vector state, numeric-conversion domains,
  backend threads and exact linked termination behavior remain unattached.
- The [Z3](retained-z3-output-capacity.md) and
  [Alt-Ergo](retained-altergo-output-capacity.md) accounts also retain diagnostic,
  occurrence, payload, command/state or termination residuals. A closed CVC5
  component alone could not supply the common solver-output bound.

Consequently the standard output/JSON composition cannot substitute numerical
values for complete per-attempt `q` or weighted `O`. An optimistic sufficiency
ceiling or an oversized conservative allowance is not a proof of actual failure.

## Alternatives and decision boundary

1. **Retain the current language and this reviewed stop.** Preserve the native
   rule and all accepted conditional evidence. Leave expansion and production
   #88–#94 unavailable under their existing prerequisite. This is the immediate
   research outcome; it neither changes membership nor declares the current
   supported implementation defective. Other work needs its own existing or
   new authorization and must preserve the current Oracle guarantees.
2. **Continue research with the same selected tools/configuration.** First prove
   the warning recurrence/payload functions or a selected-task exclusion, then
   complete the parser/terminal and other-solver interfaces. Use the audit table
   to retain the wider coverage, correspondence and composition requirements.
   A successful next local result is not an automatic whole-goal unlock. Start
   with a bounded hypothesis and an explicit checkpoint instead of repeating
   broad successful runs.
3. **Research stock configuration that reduces nonessential output.** The parser
   packet identifies a prospective `--quiet` variant: verbosity becomes -1 and
   the relevant warning channels discard bytes, while check/reason results and
   non-ok command status remain printed. This changes the selected invocation
   and has not been adopted or validated end to end. Null streams can still
   evaluate operands/rendering; raw stderr, exceptions, statistics and other
   solver output remain. Establish actual configuration routing (extra Why3
   configuration cannot simply redefine built-in provers), preservation of all
   required semantics/reports, and source/representation joins before using it.
   The alternative is not a completed remedy or proof that #119 is easy to finish.
4. **Reconsider architecture, solver/tool versions or guarantees.** Such changes
   might alter the research problem but require an explicit owner decision when
   they depart from the selected unchanged-tools scope. An Oracle amendment
   requires the owner's explicit approval. No external compiler/toolchain
   development, repinning, obligation suppression or assumption weakening is
   selected by this report. A smaller domain is useful only with its own complete
   source-derived guarantee, not an invented numerical cap.

The missing theorem is a reason to stop this open-ended derivation at its
authorized research boundary, not a proof that an unchanged-tool solution
cannot exist. The accumulated unresolved joins and the cost of successive
local closures support reassessing direction before more rounds. The round
ledger records cost evidence; token volume and document count are not progress
or evidence of soundness.
