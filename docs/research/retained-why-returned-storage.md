## Initial family and exact fields

The original keys comprise 561 Dlogic, two Dind and 675 Paxiom keys. R56's
complete Dlogic/Dind projection and R55's guarded initial-family transfer supply
the initial representatives. R51 supplies the raw/CE stored closures, including
Input's retained compatible seed alternatives. The new projection follows
every body, trigger, pattern and saved value, and inventories all bound, free,
free-cache and saved-key variables. It includes variable types even when a key
or image is unused by the opened formula, and every referenced symbol signature.

For each of the ten domain/entry-route pairs it checks:

- Every saved image is **Tvar**, not merely a one-node opened term. The maximum
  initial saved-map cardinality is six; raw Put maps have at most four.
- Every reconstructed context formula has mass M ≤ 99. Every root has at most
  P = 8 bound-variable slots including its definition formals. Pattern-bound
  slots are zero.
- All term, bound/free/cache/key/image-variable and signature types have mass
  at most five in Put_Line/Put and three elsewhere; signature arity is at most
  seven. These bounds include types hidden from ordinary formula equality.
- The six cases in each context are isolated Dlogic bodies, with two nullary
  patterns, a one-node variable scrutinee and binder-free branch bodies.
- Put's four closed epsilon occurrences are exactly the four `ult_wf`, `ugt_wf`,
  `slt_wf`, `sgt_wf` axiom heads, joined by physical prsymbol to R54 metadata.
  First has one other closed epsilon: its conjunction body has no bindings, its
  witness has two nodes and its residual property has three. The other two
  contexts have no epsilon.
- Every let has a binder-free value. Its body is binder-free, except for one
  two-let Dlogic pattern in First/Input: the inner value is exactly the outer
  bound variable, and the inner body is binder-free with two uses of its bound
  variable. Inlining choices preserve these properties.

## Producer transfer over stored representations

The invariant concerns the **stored** graph: all saved images are variable
leaves, all key and image types are bounded, and all opened bound slots are at
most P. It is stronger than strict equality. Successful weak returns select a
previously covered producer result; they do not establish the invariant by
equality alone. Immutable table values, valid free-variable caches, unique
identities, successful guarded mutation and the accepted no-force source
invariant remain explicit premises.

`bv_subst_unsafe` first restricts an incoming map to the binding's current
semantic free-variable cache. Its image composition maps every old saved
value, including dead entries, before joining the restricted map. Composing
variable images with variable images produces variable images. All new fresh
variables copy their source variable's type. `t_close_bound`, `t_close_branch`
and `t_close_quant` reset the current saved map to empty. Safe-map callbacks
may instead retain the original binding; that retains its whole old map.
Neither case drops hidden fields from the proof.

## Finite producer budget

A binding's free-variable cache has at most P keys: it describes the opened
subterm of a closed formula whose total bound/formal slots are at most P.
Dead saved keys are separately counted in K and are not counted as live free
variables. Every restricted substitution can therefore add at most P keys to
any binding it reaches, however large the incoming old saved map was.

In one complete productive traversal, at most P enclosing bound slots are
opened along a retained binding's lineage. Nullary case branches have no
nested binding and cannot add another productive opening to that lineage.
Thus a traversal adds at most P² saved keys to a surviving binding. A reset
only lowers the bound. Source transformations do not duplicate bound slots in
an original-key result: inlining has coefficients ≤ 1; epsilon and let changes
remove slots; nullary case compilation creates none; a single selected split
formula uses each original subtree at most once, including its trigger lists.
The fixed formals remain part of P.

The following deliberately conservative union covers each actual selected
preparation. It counts traversals which can contribute stored fields, not
read-only validator openings whose temporary results are discarded.

The algebraic three cover CVC5's explicit projections plus its later
compile-match/projections pair; Z3 uses the pair, Alt-Ergo uses compile-match
plus one full rewrite. Guard/general epsilon each allow an extra witness or
canonical-body traversal, although the fixed surviving bodies make it
unnecessary. Simplification allows its traversal and a substituted result;
the latter is binder-free here. Premise splitting's four cover the recursive
split, a possible drop-by/so traversal, and the selected reconstruction's
open/close work; the no-case original axioms cannot enter its compile-and-recur
case branch. The two entry/inline charges also cover partial constructors
before a later validation failure. A cache hit creates no new lineage edge.

For a process with N registered roots, there are at most E = 3N + e preparation
episodes. A provenance chain leading to any returned representation visits at
most 16E productive traversals: selecting an older object jumps backwards in
chronological construction history, and an original key is constructed at most
once per selected pass. Fresh-headed branches cannot rejoin this key. Hence

```
K = K0 + 16 E P²       K0 = 6, P = 8
R = M (1 + K V)        M = 99, V = 1
```

With variable-only images, the stored ordinary skeleton has the same constructor
shape as its fully opened tree; there are no binding containers hidden inside
an image. At most M binding positions each contribute K one-node values, giving
R including every stored edge. Strict equality is used **only now**, to attach
M = 99 to a first-inline return after the independent producer invariant has
bounded its extra fields. Intermediate later-stage representatives need not
have M = 99; the producer argument needs their P and leaf-image bounds instead.

## Validator interface and arithmetic

For an independent temporary opening/closing walk, use
`K_open = K + 2P²`, `R_open = M(1 + K_open)`. These are scratch representations;
they do not create extra preparation episodes. An entire semantic/stored walk
has at most `U = M + (P+1)R_open` term/opening entries. Binder opening traverses
the stored graph; free-variable scans are bounded by `M·U`. Including hidden
key types and symbol signatures, `(A+2)T²·U` bounds recursive type-node visits,
with A ≤ 7 and T ≤ 5/3. These are source operation categories, not native costs
for balanced-map, comparison-string, allocator or arbitrary-precision primitives.

`compose.py` lists categories totaling twenty traversal units and charges
**32 per context key per first-inline invocation**, even when branches are
mutually exclusive: mapper/definition
opening, both callback-comparison operands, closure free/type scans, termination
opening/walk, both used-symbol folds, environment opening, task match checking,
inductive prefix/positivity/type scans, declaration hashing and proposition
free-variable checking. Termination has no recursive singleton call component
in the accepted R56 family; match patterns are the fixed nullary patterns.
The 32-unit charge covers first-inline construction and returned-value
validation only; it does not charge later stages' validators. Task
foundness/positivity for Dtype/Ddata are outside these term-declaration keys and do not read an old returned term binder map.

Summed over the five processes, the additional conservative context interface
is 498,048,222,633,984 term/opening entries,
49,306,774,040,764,416 free-variable scan entries, and
108,700,861,096,599,552 type-node entries. Each is below 2^62. These overlapping
categories should not be interpreted as disjoint elapsed-time charges. Retain
R56's candidate/kernel/goal charges when composing a complete first-inline
budget; this packet permits overcharging their context portions rather than
subtracting evidence whose interfaces differ.
